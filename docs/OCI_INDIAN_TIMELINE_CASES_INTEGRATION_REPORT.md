# Indian Timeline Cases: OCI, subtype "Adult Former Indian Passport Holder"

Date: 2026-11-12

## What was built
- **New dropdown section "Indian timeline cases"** in the Admin visa dropdown, below Single applicant and above Notice responses (`Admin/frontend/src/utils/visaOptions.js`: group labels and ranks; `INDIAN_TIMELINE_VISA_TYPES`).
- **Visa type "OCI - Overseas Citizen of India"** (value `oci`, stored visa type `OCI`), single-person, no forms (`config/visaCategories.js`, `config/visaDropdown.js`, Admin copy).
- **Subtype dropdown** in the Admin "New case" form (`CreateCaseModal.jsx`): after OCI is chosen a required "Checklist type" select appears (`INDIAN_TIMELINE_SUBTYPES`); the first subtype is "OCI - Adult Former Indian Passport Holder" (with its description). Each future subtype = one entry in that map, one filing-type entry and one checklist definition.
- **Case creation = the existing single-party filing mechanism** (`single-party-filings`, registry `config/filingTypes.js`, new entry `OCI_ADULT_FORMER_INDIAN_PASSPORT`, `section: "indian_timeline"`): it creates the single-person case for the client, links the client login, and assigns the subtype's checklist to the client. The entry is excluded from the status-change/extension/EAD pickers (`groupedForSelection`) and from the scaffold generator (`singlePartyChecklists.js`).
- **Checklist** `oci_adult_former_indian_passport_checklist` (`questionnaires/ociChecklists.js`, registered in `questionnaire.service.js`): client role, **not a default** of the OCI visa type (a later subtype never receives this one), **no canonical paths, no forms** (a test checks both). The four opening instructions are the checklist description. 73 items: contact (2), A personal (6), B family incl. conditional spouse fields (13), C employment incl. conditional employer/retired fields (5), D prior OCI history (repeatable), surrender/cancellation details, relatives in India (repeatable, optional), and the document categories (digital photo, signature, physical photographs as an acknowledgement, current/last Indian passport, naturalization certificate or parents' alternative, surrender certificate when naturalized in/after 2010, legal-status evidence and pending-extension evidence for non-US-passport holders, marital-status evidence incl. remarried/deceased/affidavit, name change, employment evidence by situation, family OCI cards (optional), previous Indian visa, US address proof by type, native-address proof for non-US nationals and dual citizens).
- Client sends the answers through the ordinary questionnaire flow; Admin reads the same answer rows (no new route).

## Assumptions / limits
- Documents that "do not apply" are simply not shown (rather than a typed N/A); free-text answers accept N/A. A required document the applicant truly cannot provide has no N/A control.
- Digital photograph and signature are two separate uploads; the "send by email" wording is an instruction only.
- Physical photographs are an acknowledgement (not an upload).
- Marital status values: Single / Never Married, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Separated; "Remarried after divorce" and "certificate unavailable" are separate follow-up questions, not statuses.
- Not verified in a browser: that the client portal shows the checklist description (instructions) prominently, the single-option radio, and the repeatable-group add/remove controls exactly as in GC-NVC.
- The Questionnaires page lists the checklist under its own visa type ("OCI") via the visa options.

# Second subtype: OCI - Adult Foreign Citizen by Birth (2026-11-12)

- **Registered** like the first subtype: filing-type entry `OCI_ADULT_FOREIGN_CITIZEN_BY_BIRTH` (`config/filingTypes.js`, `section: "indian_timeline"`, visa type `OCI`), checklist `oci_adult_foreign_citizen_by_birth_checklist` (`questionnaires/ociBirthChecklist.js`, registered in `questionnaire.service.js`), added to the Admin subtype dropdown (`INDIAN_TIMELINE_SUBTYPES.oci`) and to the OCI registry `subTypes`. Not a default, no forms, no canonical paths; question keys use the prefix `oci_birth_` (no key is shared with the first subtype). The first subtype's files and definition (73 questions) are unchanged; a test checks its key and count.
- **Coverage:** the 17 document categories (digital photo + signature as two uploads; physical photographs as an acknowledgement; passport; birth certificate with an apostille/attestation choice only when born outside India; Indian-origin evidence = unlimited uploads + a repeatable ancestor/lineage group; parents' OCI/PIO cards per parent with supporting documents; legal status + pending extension; applicant's own marital evidence; spouse passport; name change; employment evidence by situation; parents' naturalization certificates and passports per parent with N/A; spouse/family OCI cards; previous Indian visa; US address proof by type; native-address proof incl. dual citizens; parents' apostilled marriage certificate) and sections A-D (root-ancestor options include Great-grandparent).
- **Rules chosen:** "applying on a parent's eligibility" = the root Indian ancestor is Father or Mother (this drives the parents' marriage certificate, not the applicant's marital status); the applicant's own marriage certificate and the parents' are separate items. Parents' cards are uploaded once under the parents' OCI/PIO item; the optional "parents' and spouse's OCI cards" item says not to upload them again (there is no automatic reuse of one upload for two items, so this is handled by wording, not by linking).
- **Tests run:** `ociBirthChecklist` (6 tests) plus the earlier named Backend files: 83 pass; Admin utils + Questionnaire page 74 pass; Admin build OK. Not run: a real case, client portal rendering, upload/save/resume end to end, organization isolation.

# Third subtype: Minor Foreign citizen and never held Indian passport (2026-11-12)

- **Name:** shown exactly as in the supplied document list ("Minor Foreign citizen and never held Indian passport"), like the other two.
- **Registered** the same way: filing-type entry `OCI_MINOR_FOREIGN_CITIZEN_NEVER_HELD` (`config/filingTypes.js`, `section: "indian_timeline"`, visa type `OCI`), checklist `oci_minor_foreign_citizen_never_held_passport_checklist` (`questionnaires/ociMinorChecklist.js`, registered in `questionnaire.service.js`, kept out of the scaffold generator), OCI registry `subTypes`, and the Admin subtype dropdown. Not a default, no forms, no canonical paths. Question keys use the prefix `oci_minor_`; none is shared with the other two subtypes. The first two checklists' definitions and saved data are untouched (a test checks their keys and the first one's 73 questions).
- **Coverage:** contact (name, phone; no guardian fields), sections A-C (A: distinguishing mark, root ancestor incl. Great-grandparent, country/place of birth, nationality, "Are you a student?" + institution name/address/phone when Yes; B: both parents; C: prior OCI applications (repeatable), surrender/cancellation, relatives in India (repeatable, optional)), and the 13 document categories: digital photograph (always required) + "Can the minor sign?" -> signature OR left thumb impression (exactly one, blue ink), physical photographs (acknowledgement, never an upload), current passport, birth certificate (+ apostille/attestation choice + self-attested copy), Indian-origin evidence (unlimited uploads + ancestor lineage group), legal status + pending extension, name change, parents' naturalization certificates and passports (per parent, N/A allowed), parents' marital evidence, parents' OCI cards (optional), previous Indian visa, US address proof by type, native-address proof incl. dual citizens.
- **Parents' marital evidence:** one status is chosen (Married / Divorced / One parent deceased). Once chosen, all three evidence items are available (so more than one circumstance can be documented); only the chosen circumstance's item is required. Authentication of a parents' marriage certificate issued abroad is not asked (the supplied text does not specify it).
- **Documented ambiguity (birth certificate):** the supplied text says a copy of the birth certificate may be provided "if born in the USA", and separately that an applicant born outside India needs an apostille/attestation and a self-attested copy. Implemented literally: the birth certificate, the authentication choice (one of two) and the self-attested copy are asked/required when the country of birth is anything other than India (USA included); nothing is asked of an applicant born in India, since the text states no rule for that case. No universal requirement was invented. Confirm with the business if an India-born minor must also provide a birth certificate.
- **Tests run:** `ociMinorChecklist` (8 tests) plus the other OCI, i130, visa-dropdown and Questionnaires-page-sync files: 44 pass; Admin utils tests and build OK. Not run: a real case, client portal rendering, upload/save/resume end to end, organization isolation.

# Fourth subtype: OCI Checklist for Spouse of Foreign Origin (2026-11-12)

- **Name:** "OCI Checklist for Spouse of Foreign Origin", the supplied document's title as it appears in your file list (the request called it "OCI - Foreign-Origin Spouse"); one string to change if you prefer another wording.

- **Coverage:** contact; A (marital status, mark, root ancestor incl. Great-grandparent, birth country/place, nationality); B (parents; the spouse-based question; spouse category dropdown; spouse name, date of birth, passport number / issue / expiry / place, address, nationality, occupation; OCI number / issue date / place only for an OCI cardholder; marriage date; India-or-abroad; registration question for India / apostille question for abroad - never both; still subsisting; more than one living spouse and the spouse's other living spouse, each with its repeatable group where the OCI card number is optional); C (employment); D (prior OCI applications, surrender, relatives); and documents 5.1-5.13 (digital photograph and signature as two uploads, physical photographs as an acknowledgement, applicant's passport, applicant's birth certificate with the country of ISSUANCE asked and one-of-two authentication when issued outside India, ancestors' birth certificates as a repeatable lineage record + uploads, marriage certificate with an optional "not in English" question that requires the translation, spouse evidence package by category, legal status + pending extension, name change, employment evidence, previous Indian visa, US address proof, native-address proof incl. dual citizens).
- **Staff review (no automatic decisions):** answers that may disqualify - marital status other than Married, "No" to the spouse-based question, a marriage no longer subsisting, more than one living spouse, the spouse's other living spouse - carry `metadata.staffReview`. The Admin answers panel now shows an amber "Needs staff review: ..." note under such an answer (new `staffReviewNotes` in `QuestionnaireAnswersPanel.jsx`; it only appears when a question has these rules, so every other checklist is unchanged). The client sees a non-blocking warning on marital status other than Married. The subtype and eligibility are never changed automatically.
- **Date checks:** a spouse's date of birth, passport issue date, OCI issue date and marriage date must be in the past (existing "date past" rule). "Expiry after issue" is shown as guidance only - the existing rules cannot compare two fields.
- **Count note:** the request says 12 document categories but lists 13 sections (5.1-5.13); all 13 are implemented.
